# ADR-004 — Unresolved hosting, retention and regulatory classification

**DESIGN-ONLY / NOT APPROVED** | Status: DRAFT / PROPOSED; expert decisions PENDING | 2026-11-15, Asia/Calcutta

P00 and full P01 remain **BLOCKED**. This unresolved ADR does not authorize hosting, retention defaults or a regulatory classification.

## Assumptions and dependencies

No accountable operator, approved region/provider, lawful processing inventory, retention schedule, current applicability assessment or software/CDS classification. APR-01/APR-02/APR-03/APR-04/APR-05/APR-08 remain PENDING; H05/H07/H10 OPEN. No data store, backup, infrastructure or service is provisioned.

## Context

A hosting brand or architectural diagram cannot establish lawful health/genetic processing. Remote administration, identity/notification/model suppliers, backups and support access may create additional locations/transfers. Retention applies to derived outputs/audits/backups, and withdrawal does not settle every legal deletion obligation. The earlier official-source check did not establish current full legal text or applicability.

## Proposed decision

Leave hosting/residency, remote access, retention/deletion, controller/operator responsibilities and regulatory classification explicitly unresolved. Do not select a cloud region, legal exception, retention duration or clinical device status in this design. Require counsel/operator/privacy/security decisions linked to the exact activity/flow and authoritative current evidence before real collection or deployment.

Use [D01-D09 and F01-F09](../data-flows.md) as the review inventory. Include suppliers, geographic access, key custody, archival, incident reporting, rights and contracts in the later assessment. No numerical notification deadline or legal retention period is assumed here.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

- Pick a provider/region now: enables concrete infrastructure planning but risks treating an unapproved assumption as compliance; defer.
- Pick uniform deletion on withdrawal: easy messaging but may conflict with prior disclosure/evidence duties; defer to approved category-specific decisions.
- Explicit pending decisions: blocks production and full P01 acceptance but preserves accurate governance and prevents fabricated clearance.

## Consequences and review gaps

Architecture options can be reviewed as provisional under the restricted exception; real data, deployment, integrations and implementation remain unauthorized. Lawful purpose/owner/retention gates for full P01 remain unmet. Source/approval gaps D01/D02 remain open. Consult [regulatory assessment](../regulatory-applicability.md), [governance register](../governance-approval-register.md) and [risk register](../risk-register.md); none is a compliance certification. Reviewer identities and acceptance PENDING.
