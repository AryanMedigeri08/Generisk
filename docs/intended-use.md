# Intended use and exclusions

Version: P00 draft 0.1 | Date: 2026-11-15 (Asia/Calcutta) | Approval: NOT APPROVED

## Explicit intended-use statement

Amanah Genetics is proposed as an Arabic/English educational and care-navigation service for independently participating adults in Abu Dhabi. Subject to future operator, professional, privacy and regulatory authorization, it may present narrowly scoped, clinician-reviewed inherited-disease risk information and facilitate separately authorized joint review and referral to qualified genetics professionals. It is not intended to diagnose disease, determine variant pathogenicity, prescribe treatment, decide whether people should marry or replace professional counselling or required screening. The current repository contains planning documentation only and is restricted to synthetic-only development.

Risk is not diagnosis. Screening information has scope and limitations; absence of a finding must not be presented as absence of all inherited disease risk. No numerical risk estimates, disease rules or clinically approved educational corpus are supplied in P00.

## Three distinct activities

| Activity | Allowed boundary for a future authorized product | Required oversight |
|---|---|---|
| General education | Explain approved general concepts and how to seek support without interpreting an individual's findings | Versioned clinical approval and Arabic/English review of each content item |
| Clinician-reviewed decision support | Display reviewed, traceable output of deterministic rules within an approved disease/test scope; preserve uncertainty and amendments | Qualified professional reviews input provenance and output; counsel evaluates regulatory classification |
| Clinical advice | Individual diagnosis, treatment or reproductive management decisions | Outside autonomous product/LLM scope; delivered only by appropriately authorized professionals under the operator's approved clinical service |

Calling a function educational does not determine its legal classification. Claims, marketing, inputs, outputs and actual use require counsel/operator review. Disclaimers alone do not authorize clinical activity.

## User choice and joint review

Participation and processing consent do not automatically authorize partner disclosure. Each adult must independently authorize the specific joint review and understand that shared conclusions can reveal information about either person. Refusal must not expose private findings or be framed as relationship failure. Withdrawal must block future access/processing in accordance with approved policy; it cannot erase knowledge already lawfully seen or automatically override statutory retention. Detailed consent rules, retention and permissions are P01 work, pending review.

## Prohibited claims and output

- No marriage-compatibility binary labels, relationship rankings, genetic superiority labels or recommendation to marry/separate.
- No autonomous diagnosis, pathogenicity inference, treatment, reproductive directives or prediction outside approved deterministic logic.
- No unsupported numerical risk, universal reassurance, fabricated source, or assertion that a simulated referral is a verified appointment.
- No personal/genetic data in prompts, embeddings, public repositories or telemetry; no real report upload during synthetic development.

## Missing knowledge, uncertainty and escalation

If approved educational knowledge is missing, use a reviewed static fallback or abstain: knowledge is not yet available and a qualified professional should be consulted. This English fallback is a draft for review, not an approved content asset. No Arabic clinical copy is approved. The LLM must not generate an answer to fill gaps or override deterministic/professional output.

Ambiguous, incomplete, corrected or unsupported reports must stop automated interpretation and be referred to a qualified reviewer in a future authorized service. Suspected coercion, unauthorized access or harmful interpretation must suspend disclosure and invoke the approved safety pathway. The service is not an emergency or crisis service; clinical owners must approve local escalation content before any real use.

## Conditions for expansion

No real data, medical claims, recruitment, real integration or deployment before the relevant approvals in [the applicability register](regulatory-applicability.md) are evidenced. Changes to population, diseases, test scope, content, model, rules or sharing purposes require renewed safety/legal review and versioned acceptance. P00 does not select a regulatory classification or claim compliance.
