# Regulatory applicability assessment

Version: P00 draft 0.1 | Date checked: 2026-11-15 (Asia/Calcutta) | Status: UNRESOLVED; NOT LEGAL ADVICE OR COMPLIANCE CERTIFICATION

## Assessment boundary and source limitations

This is a preliminary question matrix for a proposed Abu Dhabi educational/joint-review service. It does not decide legal applicability, medical-device status, licensure, lawful basis or permission to process genetic information. Synthetic documentation is the only current activity. The accountable operator and UAE counsel are unassigned.

Official search results confirmed instrument titles and the ADHICS V2 document-control metadata. Direct full-text retrieval failed or returned no readable body. No full instrument, Arabic controlling text, amendments, implementation guidelines or current applicability matrix was fully inspected. [Source-check evidence](evidence/regulatory-source-check.md) records what was and was not available. No assertion is made that these sources are exhaustive or unsuperseded; expert re-verification is required.
